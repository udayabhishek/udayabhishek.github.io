03 · ENGINEERING PRINCIPLES

How I approach engineering problems.

Simple principles guide the way I design, implement and maintain production software.

01

Keep responsibilities clear

Use separation of concerns and appropriate abstractions so features remain easier to understand, test and evolve.

02

Design for testability

Keep business logic independent from infrastructure where practical, making important behaviour straightforward to verify.

03

Measure before optimizing

Use real production signals and profiling rather than adding complexity without evidence of a performance problem.

04

Use concurrency deliberately

Prefer structured concurrency and clear ownership of mutable state to build responsive and predictable applications.

05

Prefer maintainable solutions

Favor readable, focused designs that the team can understand and safely change over unnecessary complexity.
